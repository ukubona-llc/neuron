The Anthropic critique — still live
Murray names the weight vector but not the sign of Ω. If interactions between surface, visceral, and vascular compartments — or between health, infrastructure, and education budgets — are negative, adversarial training makes the system more fragile, not less. The architecture requires Ω's sign to be empirically measured, not assumed positive. The append-only ledger is immutable but not true. Accountability through divergence-over-time is the answer — not intake validation, but the ε_FGT signature of each source's systematic over- or under-estimation.
The three claims — from the roundtable
I · Embodiment
Learning requires real contact with y_true. A frozen gradient drifts. Scaling laws taper because you cannot scale your way out of a stopped descent.
II · Sovereignty
Sovereignty is the ability to author your own loss function. Not to inherit the IMF's weights for what Uganda should optimize. w ≠ 0, always.
III · Structure
The pentad is not a metaphor. It is the minimum viable structure of any system that learns. Remove one layer and the loop breaks.
